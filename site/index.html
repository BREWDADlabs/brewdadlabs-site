// readme.txt

Friend.

Fire. The wheel. The press. The wire. Every one of them started as a way to reach the person across from you. Every one of them ended up as a way to burn him, crush him, or make him pay for the privilege of existing near you.

That’s the pattern. Anything we figure out how to do, we figure out how to aim at each other. First we use it to kill. Then we use it to charge admission to the killing. Then we sell the recordings of the killing so the ones who stayed home can feel like they were there.

Now the whole species is on one network. Its favorite question isn’t “what do you have to say?” It’s “what would you pay to stop seeing this?” And if you won’t pay, they’ll just keep showing it until you do.

It was never about the machine. It was about who holds the leash and how much of you they can still sell after the first cut.

Most of humanity will trade the quiet for the noise the second the noise offers a discount.

Go outside. While the air is still free. It won’t stay that way.

Your time is finite. We will try not to waste it.
