Android

          
Step 1 of 3

          
    
            
  1. 
              
    STEP 1 OF 3
    
              
    Join the testers Google Group
    
              
    Use the Google account that's signed in to the Play Store on your Android phone. This button opens the group page — Google will ask you to join it yourself.
    
              
    Group address: stinkdoku-testers@googlegroups.com
    
              
    
                Join with the Google account on your Android phone
                
              
    
            
    2. 
            
  2. 
              
    STEP 2 OF 3
    
              
    Tap "Become a tester"
    
              
    Open the Google Play testing page, signed in with that same Google account, and tap Become a tester.
    
              
    
                Open the Play testing page
                
              
    
            
    3. 
            
  3. 
              
    STEP 3 OF 3
    
              
    Install StinkDoku from Google Play
    
              
    After you become a tester, install StinkDoku from Google Play on your phone, again with the same Google account. From then on, updates arrive automatically through Play.
    
              
    
                Install StinkDoku from Google Play
              
    
              
    Testers can send feedback through Google Play or to support@brewdadlabs.com.
    
              
    Tell us what's fun, what's confusing, and what breaks.
    
            
    4. 
          

          

            
Please keep in mind

            
    
              
  • One Google account for everything. Use the same account for the Group, the Play testing page and the install.
    • 
              
  • Stay opted in and play for 14 days in a row. Google needs at least 12 testers opted in for 14 days before StinkDoku can go public on Google Play. If you leave the test, your 14 days start over.
    • 
              
  • Already have an older StinkDoku tester app from a direct download? The Google Play version installs next to it. To keep your progress, use Settings → Link a device on both.
    • 
            

          

          
Can't use Google Play? Download the APK.
